# Print Perfect Pro

Create a modern, clean, and highly professional website for a Printing & Packaging Company, inspired by the sleek digital catalog and corporate aesthetic of univerzlk.com.

### 🎨 Design & Aesthetic Guidelines:

- Color Palette: Clean white/light gray background with modern high-contrast primary colors (e.g., Deep Navy Blue and Vibrant Accent Orange/Cyan) representing precision and creativity.

- Layout: Modern grid layout, smooth animations, fully responsive for mobile and desktop, card-based service highlights, and clean typography.

### 📑 Key Pages & Sections:

1. Hero Section:

   - Dynamic headline: "Bringing Your Ideas to Life with Precision Printing"

   - Subheadline showcasing full-service printing, custom packaging, and branding solutions.

   - Dual Call-to-Action (CTA) buttons: "Get Instant Quote" and "Explore Services".

   - High-quality hero showcase banner/graphic.

2. Interactive Product & Service Catalog:

   - Categorized grid/tabs:

     - Commercial Printing (Business cards, Flyers, Brochures, Letterheads)

     - Packaging Solutions (Custom boxes, Labels, Paper bags, Product sleeves)

     - Large Format & Signage (Banners, Vinyl stickers, Acrylic boards, Display stands)

     - Customized Merchandise (T-shirts, Mugs, Caps, Corporate gifts)

   - Product Cards: Include high-res image placeholders, title, quick spec highlights, and a "Request Quote" button for each.

3. "Instant Quote Request" Interactive Tool:

   - A step-by-step or clean single-page estimation form where clients can:

     - Select Product Category & Type.

     - Select Quantity, Paper/Material Type, Finishing (Matte/Gloss/Lamination).

     - File Upload field for design artwork (PDF, AI, PNG).

     - Contact details field and "Submit Request" button.

4. Portfolio / Gallery Section:

   - Filterable gallery showcasing past completed projects (Packaging, Banners, Corporate Branding).

   - Lightbox modal view when a project image is clicked.

5. Why Choose Us (Feature Highlights):

   - Fast Turnaround & Delivery

   - Premium Quality Print & Materials

   - Eco-Friendly Inks & Options

   - Competitive Wholesale Pricing

6. Testimonials & Client Logos:

   - Social proof section with customer feedback sliders and logos of corporate partners.

7. Contact & Sticky Quick Actions:

   - Contact form with Google Maps location placeholder.

   - Contact info (Phone, Email, Address, Business Hours).

   - Floating WhatsApp Chat button in the bottom right corner.

   - Clean Footer with quick site links, social media icons, and copyright.

### 🛠 Tech & UX Features:

- Smooth scrolling and subtle hover interactions on buttons and cards.

- Mobile-first responsive navigation bar with a slick mobile drawer menu.
